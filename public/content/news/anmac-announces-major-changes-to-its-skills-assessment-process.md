The Australian Nursing and Midwifery Accreditation Council (ANMAC) has announced upcoming changes to its Skilled Migration Services, including a new Skilled Migration Assessment Portal, updated skills assessment types and names, revised assessment fees and a new agent declaration process.

The changes are expected to take effect when the new portal launches. ANMAC has not yet confirmed the launch date and says further information will be provided before the transition.

Here are the key changes ANMAC has announced as part of the upcoming update to its Skilled Migration Services:

## New Skilled Migration Assessment Portal

ANMAC will introduce a new Skilled Migration Assessment Portal for submitting, tracking and managing skills assessment applications.

The new portal is designed to provide a more secure, streamlined and user-friendly experience for applicants, migration agents and agencies.

Migration agents and agencies will be able to manage applications through dedicated profiles. Agencies will also be able to add and manage staff and view the progress of client applications from a central dashboard.

## Updated ANMAC Skills Assessment Types and Names

ANMAC is also changing the names and types of its skills assessment services. The updated names are intended to provide greater clarity about the assessment service being provided.

The new assessment types are:

### Essential Skills Assessment

This assessment is for applicants who are currently registered as a nurse or midwife in Australia. It assesses the applicant's qualifications only.

### Essential Plus Skills Assessment

This assessment is for applicants who are currently registered as a nurse or midwife in Australia or New Zealand and want both their qualifications and work experience assessed.

This may be relevant where an applicant needs to meet specific ANZSCO code requirements or minimum points requirements for an Australian skilled visa.

### Comprehensive Skills Assessment

This assessment is for applicants who are registered as a nurse or midwife in an approved overseas country, meet ANMAC's eligibility requirements and do not currently hold nursing or midwifery registration in Australia or New Zealand.

### Care Skills Assessment

The Care skills assessment supports the Australian Government's Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement (ACILA).

Applicants can apply to have either their qualification criteria or work experience criteria assessed.

### Care Plus Skills Assessment

The Care Plus skills assessment supports the Aged Care Industry Labour Agreement (ACILA) and Designated Area Migration Agreement (DAMA) pathways.

Applicants can apply to have both their qualification and work experience criteria assessed.

## Revised ANMAC Skills Assessment Fees

ANMAC has revised its skills assessment fees to better reflect the complexity of assessment activities and improve transparency for applicants.

The revised fees will apply when the updated services are introduced through the new portal. However, ANMAC has not yet confirmed the portal launch date.

## New Agent Declaration Process

ANMAC will also introduce a new electronic agent declaration process.

Applicants who use a migration agent will need to provide electronic consent before the agent can submit an application on their behalf.

This will form part of the new portal process and is intended to provide a clearer and more secure way of managing applicant-agent authorisation.

## What Will Happen to Existing ANMAC Applications?

Applicants who already have an ANMAC skills assessment application in progress will not need to start the process again when the new portal launches.

ANMAC has confirmed that applications still in progress will be transitioned to the new system.

If the assessment fee for an existing application has already been paid, the revised fee will not apply to that application. There will also be no additional fee because of the transition to the new portal.

Migration agents nominated on existing applications will receive an activation email with instructions on how to access the application through the new portal.

Applicants will also not need to reapply or resubmit their documents solely because of the transition.

ANMAC will provide further information about the transition process before the new portal launches.
